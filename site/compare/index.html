0 · The room

Before, and with Norwegian restraint

Before is the EDS baseline you have seen so far: cool white by day, near-black by night, no motif. The Nordic options change only the room around the data: surfaces that are warm or cool white by day, mørketid by night (deep blue-grey, never black, so the one warm highlight glows like a lamp in a window), and the horizon motif. Data colours, type and rules are identical in all six panels. Use Room in the top bar to apply one to the whole page.

The horizon motif here is an illustrative profile. In stories it is drawn from real bathymetry or an interpreted seismic horizon, and sits outside plot areas.
