This page demonstrates the SubScout story format. Every number in the text is computed from the data
when the page is built: hover one to see its exact value, and find all of them in
[How we know](#how-we-know). The basin, its {{ n_fields }} fields and its operators are invented.

::: step {beat=and}
### Most fields peak early, then fade

Grey is context. One field is in colour because it behaves differently: it held its plateau for
{{ longest_plateau }} years, against a typical {{ typical_plateau }}.

```chart
id: production
type: line
data: production
x: year
y: kboed
series: field
volume: true
focus: [Field C]
unit_label: "k {unit}/d"
title: Field C held its plateau for {{ longest_plateau }} years
subtitle: Production, thousand Sm³ o.e. per day (switch units in the top bar)
source: synthetic
confidence: observed
unit: k Sm³ o.e./d; 1 Sm³ o.e. = 6.29 boe
```
:::

::: step {beat=and}
### A tie-back extended the pattern

A second story colour joins, and a highlight marks the moment that matters. The lines keep their
identity; only their emphasis changes. Field C still provides {{ share_2025_c | pct }} of the
basin's output in 2025.

```chart
use: production
focus: [Field C, Field F]
note: { name: Field F, x: 2016, text: "Tie-back to Field C" }
title: Fields C and F outlasted their neighbours
```
:::

::: step {beat=but}
### But the finds got smaller

The busiest decade brought {{ peak_decade_finds }} discoveries, yet the average find has shrunk
roughly {{ size_drop | round:0 }}-fold since the first decade.

```chart
id: finds
type: bars
data: decades
label: decade
value: avg_msm3
title: The average find is a fraction of what it was
subtitle: Average discovery size by decade, million Sm³ o.e.
source: synthetic
confidence: derived
notes: Sizes are illustrative and rounded.
```
:::

::: step {beat=therefore}
### Therefore: small, near infrastructure, and price-sensitive

The average 2020s discovery holds about {{ avg_2020s | volume }}. Finds that size only pay
as tie-backs, and drilling falls hard when prices do: quarterly wells fell
{{ wells_drop | pct }} after 2015.

```chart
use: finds
highlight: [2020s]
title: A 2020s find is too small to stand alone
```
:::

## The detail

The drilling record shows the price cycle as pale bands: the 2015–16 and 2020 troughs.

```chart
id: wells
type: heat
data: wells
x: year
y: quarter
value: wells
rows: [Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4]
value_label: wells
title: Drilling follows the oil price
subtitle: Exploration wells per quarter
source: synthetic
confidence: observed
```

The fields, ranked by how long they held their plateau:
